About This Session

Stop the reflex — protect the margin

Join the Veterinary Business Institute for an ownership and leadership panel on the single biggest margin destroyer in a cost-sensitive market: the instinct to discount. With 81% of vets reporting clients more price-sensitive in 2026 than 2025, the pressure to cut prices is everywhere — and it quietly erodes the practice's bottom line.

We'll unpack the hidden math of discounting, the difference between intentional access-to-care decisions and reflexive front-desk caving, and the concrete tools — payment plans, wellness plans, CareCredit framing, and value scripts — that top owners use to protect price without losing the client relationship.

This Panel Is For You If...

  • You own, lead, or manage a veterinary practice
  • Your front desk is discounting to "save the appointment"
  • Margin is slipping but visit counts haven't crashed
  • Your team confuses access-to-care with caving on price
  • You want scripts your CSRs and associates will actually use
Meet Your Panelists

Learn From The Best

Kate Boatright, VMD
Spectrum of Care & Mentorship Advocate · Founder, KMB Veterinary Media
Dr. Kate Boatright is a practicing small-animal veterinarian, writer, and educator passionate about advancing practical, compassionate, and accessible veterinary care. With over a decade in general practice and emergency medicine, her clinical interests span feline medicine, preventive care, internal medicine, surgery, and emergency care. A nationally recognized author published in dvm360 and Today's Veterinary Practice, she is the author of the Veterinary Mentorship Manual and a frequent speaker at national conferences on spectrum of care, mentorship, and career sustainability.
Genevieve Frederick
Founder & President · Feeding Pets of the Homeless
Since 2006, Genevieve Frederick has researched homelessness and the human-animal bond, speaking with countless people experiencing homelessness about the importance of their pets. In 2008 she founded Feeding Pets of the Homeless — the first national nonprofit dedicated exclusively to providing pet food and emergency veterinary care to pets of people experiencing homelessness. Under her leadership the organization has helped keep thousands of pets healthy and together with their owners through difficult times.
